Garaje: browse, design and drive any vehicle

docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-garaje-design.html·UI design, not started·issue #83

The Garaje is a main-menu mode next to the Cuba map. It shows every vehicle the game can build on a turntable, lets the player change its shape, colours and livery inside the ranges the street traffic uses, and drives any of them into free drive. This page proposes its two screens, the rules behind the sliders and how it joins the rest of the game, with mockups you can use.

01What this delivers

Salón

Every vehicle the fleet builds, by family: cars, buses, trucks, services and cycles, plus your own designs. The selected one turns on a garage floor; drag to turn it yourself. A data card gives its era, its size and where it drives in the city.

Taller

One vehicle's parameters as rows: shape, front and rear, wheels, paint. Every slider stops at the edges of its family's range over all eras, and a slider whose limit depends on another (the wheelbase on the length) shows the part it cannot reach. The vehicle rebuilds on the turntable as you move it.

Drive it

Enter on any vehicle, in either screen, opens free drive in that vehicle. Free drive gets a Vehículo row that opens the Garaje to choose, and the pause menu gets Cambiar vehículo.

03How it fits

The Garaje adds no geometry. It reads the ranges table, builds through the same manifoldx builders as the traffic, and hands a parameter set to free drive.

04The main menu entry

Garaje goes between Mapa de Cuba and Opciones, with its one-line description, in the style of the approved menu (docs/ux/contravia-ux.html, section Menú principal). It is marked NUEVO until the player first opens it. The background turns the last vehicle the player drove or designed.

05The Salón: browse

The Salón opens on the family of the vehicle last chosen. Tabs across the top are the families; under the stage, a strip holds that family's vehicles. The stage is a garage floor with the vehicle turning slowly; dragging turns it by hand and stops the slow turn until the player lets go. The card on the right has what a player wants to know before driving it: the era, the size, the seats, how many variants of it the traffic draws, and where in the city it drives.

Actions: Manejar opens free drive in it, Diseñar opens the Taller on it, Al azar shows another variant the traffic would draw from the same era. Cycles show Manejar as pronto until riders exist (their own spec).

06The Taller: design

The Taller opens on the vehicle chosen in the Salón, as a copy: nothing the player does changes the preset. On the left, tabs group the parameters (Forma, Frente y cola, Ruedas, Pintura) and each parameter is a row: a slider with its value and its range's ends, or a set of options. On the right the vehicle turns, with camera buttons for side, front, three-quarter and rear. At the top, the era chips reset the vehicle to that era's preset; they limit nothing.

Under the rows: Al azar draws a full set of parameters from the current ranges, as the traffic would; Deshacer steps back; Guardar saves to Mis diseños; Manejar drives it now without saving.

Allowed ranges

Every slider has two kinds of limit, and the row shows both.

  • The family's range. Its ends, printed under the slider, are the lowest and highest over the family's eras in ranges(family, era), the table the fleet generator draws from. Every era's style options are open too.
  • Constraints between parameters. The generator already fixes each draw so it builds: the wheelbase fits inside the length, the heights stay in order (sill, belt, cowl, roof), the wheels fit their arches. In the Taller the same rules narrow a slider live: the slider stops at the narrowed end and the row says why in orange (try a short modern car and a long wheelbase). Moving the length back frees it.
  • A parameter a family does not have (fins on a bus) is not shown.

So ranges() returns, per family and era and parameter: its group and label in Spanish, its unit, min, max and step, and for options the list allowed in the era. The constraints live in one function, fix(params), which the generator already needs; the Taller calls it to get the narrowed ends.

Colours and livery

The Pintura tab has the body colour, a second colour (roof and livery), the two-tone roof switch and the livery preset. A service preset brings its own colours (Policía sets white over blue), which the player can then change. The swatches are every era's colour list from the fleet generator, already faded the way the traffic paints. A colour costs no build time: as the vehicles spec decided, colourways recolour one built mesh.

Rebuilding on every change

A shape change rebuilds the vehicle through its builder at the detail the traffic uses, then merges it. The time this takes per family is not measured yet; step 1 of delivery measures it. If a rebuild takes more than about 100 ms, the turntable keeps the last mesh while a slider is held and rebuilds when it is released, so the slider never stutters. Builds are cached by their parameters through contravia/kept.py, so undo and going back to a preset cost nothing.

07Saved designs

Guardar writes one file per design to <config>/garaje/<slug>.json, in the folder the options already use ($CONTRAVIA_CONFIG in tests). The name defaults to Mi plus the base vehicle's name and a number, and can be edited in the title. Mis diseños in the Salón lists them, newest first; on a design the card adds Renombrar, Duplicar and Borrar. Designs never drive in the city's traffic.

{
  "name": "Mi almendrón 2",
  "family": "car",
  "base": "almendron",
  "base_era": "1950s",
  "params": {"length": 5.2, "height": 1.52, "wheelbase": 3.0, "fin_height": 0.18,
             "wheel_radius": 0.38, "headlamp": "round_pod", "bumper": "chrome_bar",
             "wheel": "hubcap", "whitewall": 0.05},
  "paint": {"body": "#5ec8c0", "second": "#efe6cf", "roof_paint": true, "livery": "franja"},
  "saved": "2026-11-18T12:00:00"
}

A design keeps only the parameters the player could change; the rest come from the base preset when it is built. A file whose values fall outside today's ranges (a range was tightened after it was saved) is clamped with fix() on load, and the Salón marks it ajustado.

08Driving any vehicle

  • From the Garaje. Manejar opens Modo libre with this vehicle in its Vehículo row and the other rows as the player last left them; Enter there starts.
  • From Modo libre. A new Vehículo row shows the chosen vehicle's name; Enter on it opens the Salón, and choosing returns to the row.
  • While driving. The pause menu gets Cambiar vehículo: it opens the Salón over the paused city, and the new vehicle appears where the old one stood, facing the same way.
  • How it drives. The vehicle physics of M1 reads its mass, wheelbase, track and wheel size from the built vehicle, so a longer wheelbase turns wider and a bus is heavy. The Garaje's card shows no speed or handling numbers until that physics exists.

10Delivery

The Garaje waits on the fleet generator of the vehicles spec (#42, its steps 7 and 8), because that is where the ranges are written. After that, five steps, each its own PR:

1

ranges() and fix() in rules/fleet.py

Per family and era: group, label, unit, min, max, step, options. The generator moves onto them. Measure a rebuild per family at traffic detail and write the numbers in the PR.

2

The Salón and the menu entry

contravia/garage.py: tabs, strip, turntable on a floor, the card, Manejar into free drive. --screen garage --shot saves it.

3

The Taller

Rows built from ranges(), live narrowing from fix(), rebuild with the cache, undo, Al azar, the camera buttons.

4

Saved designs

JSON in the config folder, Mis diseños, rename, duplicate, delete, clamp on load.

5

Free drive and pause

The Vehículo row, Cambiar vehículo in pause, the swap in place. Then the two screens go into docs/ux/contravia-ux.html as approved UX.

11Testing

  • Every parameter at its min and its max over the family's eras, for every family, builds a vehicle whose wheels sit in their arches and whose heights are in order (a sweep over ranges()).
  • A thousand random draws inside the ranges, passed through fix(), all build; fix() of a valid set returns it unchanged.
  • A saved design loads into the same parameters and builds the same mesh; a design out of range is clamped and marked.
  • The Salón and the Taller render to PNG with --screen garage --shot and --screen garage-design --shot, and the shots are looked at in review.
  • Manejar from the Salón, from the Taller and from pause starts free drive in the chosen vehicle (driven by manifoldx emit, checked through engine.state).

12Non-goals and open questions

Non-goals

  • The workshop of the story (#72): money, parts that change the handling, damage repair. It can reuse the Taller's rows later.
  • Free painting, decals drawn by hand, or a free colour picker.
  • Sharing designs online.
  • Designs in the city's traffic.
  • Riding cycles before the people spec gives them a rider.

Open questions

  • The Moskvich's name: the mockup calls it moscovita; which of the names in use should it take?
  • Polaco and huevito have no preset yet: they join the Carros tab when the fleet generator gets them.
  • Unlocking: everything open from the start, or does the story unlock vehicles here as you get them?
