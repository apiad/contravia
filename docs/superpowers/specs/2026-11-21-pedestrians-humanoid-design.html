Pedestrians: a parametric humanoid built like CarParams

docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-pedestrians-humanoid-design.html·approved, not started

manifoldx gains HumanParams: named body measurements with ranges in, one low-poly, continuously skinned, dressed and animated figure out, the way CarParams gives a car. Its shape comes once from a klein drawing turned into a mesh by Pixal3D and read as cross-sections along each bone; after that every body is code. Contravía uses the same figure for the people on its sidewalks, its NPCs and the player on foot, with the care its vehicles get.

04How it fits

The humanoid lives in manifoldx as modeling/assets/human.py beside car.py, with the section tables under modeling/assets/data/. Contravía imports it as it imports CarParams.

05The section table

A base body is read once into a table. Each chain is a joint-to-joint axis with stations along it; each station holds a centre offset and K radii by angle. The spike used K = 16 and these chains:

  • torso: from just under the crotch to the base of the neck, vertical, 40 stations.
  • leg_l, leg_r: hip to ankle along the leg's own centre, read from a knee slice, 24 stations.
  • foot_l, foot_r: heel to toe along z at ankle height, 8 to 12 stations.
  • arm_l, arm_r: shoulder to fingertip along the arm's principal axis, 26 to 30 stations; the hand is the end of this chain, fingers together.
  • head (new): a 12 by 16 latitude-longitude grid of radii around the head centre, its bottom row the neck ring.

Every station also records its position along the bone as a fraction, so the table is independent of the base body's height. The table stores the joint positions the extraction used, which become the rest pose. The A-pose is the rest pose: arms 40 degrees from the torso and legs apart, which is what the klein drawings were asked for and what keeps armpit and crotch sections apart.

The extraction is a numpy script in manifoldx (tools/human_sections.py): read the GLB with manifoldx's own glTF reader, keep the outer shell (rays from six sides, the component hit first: Pixal3D adds an inner shell and loose bits), slice by plane, pick the section polygon nearest the chain's axis, sample K radii. Both base bodies must be read with the same K and the same station counts, or build cannot interpolate them.

06One continuous skin

The torso ring is laid out in fixed index ranges: front, back, a left side and a right side. Each limb joins in a fixed patch:

  • Crotch. Below the last full torso ring, the ring's left and right halves each close into the top ring of one leg, and the gap between them is a fixed fan of triangles across the crotch. The leg's top rings are read from the same-side half of the pelvis, so the shape is continuous.
  • Armpit and shoulder. The torso rings that span the shoulder leave out a fixed run of side indices; the arm's first ring is stitched to that opening by a bridging strip whose vertices are interpolated between the torso's side and the arm ring.
  • Neck. The head grid's bottom row has K vertices, the same as the neck's top ring.

Because index ranges are fixed, the stitch is computed once per base topology and reused for every figure. The builder decides the exact index layout and the number of bridging rings; the test is that the mesh is closed and its face count is the same for every HumanParams value.

07HumanParams

A dataclass on ParamsRanges, its names in body terms, with ranges a person reads. Lengths map to manifoldx.humanoid.Proportions (ANSUR II fractions of stature), girths scale radii by region. A first set, to be tuned in the build:

Each girth parameter weighs its rings with a smooth curve along the chain, never a step: the spike's hips showed a visible ledge where a scale changed between two rings.

08Clothes, hair and face

  • Clothes are offset rings. A shirt, trousers or a skirt is a copy of the body's rings over a span of stations, pushed out along the ring normals by a few millimetres plus a looseness profile. Sleeve and leg length are station fractions. A skirt below the crotch is one tube around both legs, read from the pelvis ring and widened down. Clothes skin with the body's weights.
  • Body under clothes is cut. Rings fully covered by an opaque garment are dropped from the body, which keeps the triangle count near the bare body's and stops skin from showing through at bends.
  • Colours by zone, as livery does: a garment has a base colour from PALETTE and optional stripes or a print band by station range. A guayabera, a school uniform, a work shirt are presets.
  • Hair and hats are small parts on the head joint: a few styles (short, long, bun, cap, straw hat), each a shell over the head grid's upper rows.
  • Shoes replace the foot chain's rings with a slightly larger sole profile.
  • The face is one decal on the head grid's front: eyes, brows and mouth as flat shapes, as the car's lamps are outlines.

The people already drawn for the game (assets/reference/: jubilado, estudiante, manisero, señora con jaba and four more) are the target presets: each becomes a HumanParams and an outfit.

09Blended skinning

skinning.py moves each vertex by one joint today. It gains a second joint index and a weight per vertex: the vertex shader blends the two transformed positions and normals. The storage blocks stay as they are (MAX_JOINTS = 32 matrices per entity). Rigid geometry keeps working: one joint with weight 1.

Weights come from the table, not from painting: a ring's weight between its two joints follows its position along the bone, 1 on the bone and falling to 0.5 at the joint over a blend length set per joint (longer at the shoulder and hip). The crotch fan and the shoulder strip take their weights from the rings they bridge.

Joints: pelvis, spine, chest, neck, head, and per side shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle, toe: 19. The humanoid skeleton today has 15; chest, head and the toes are added.

10Animation

  • Idle: breathing on the spine and chest, a weight shift every few seconds.
  • Walk and run: hip, knee and ankle flex as phase curves, arms swinging opposite, pelvis bob and twist. Step length and cadence follow stature and speed, so feet do not slide.
  • Cross: a walk with a head turn toward the traffic before the kerb.

Cycles are evaluated on the CPU into the joint blocks each frame for figures near the camera; far figures step at a lower rate. Levels and their update rates are in section 11.

11Detail and distance

Figures scale their cost the way cars do. CarParams.detail scales sections and segment counts; the graveyard (graveyard.py) switches tomb meshes by distance bands that come from the quality preset (grave_lod), with hysteresis. Pedestrians use both.

levelmeshmotion
0 nearK = 16 radii, every station, head grid 12 by 16, clothes as their own rings, face decalfull cycle every frame, two weights per vertex
1 midK = 8, every second station, head grid 6 by 8, clothes painted onto the body rings instead of offset, no facecycle at a lower rate
2 farK = 5, a few stations per chain, hair and hat merged into the heada few key poses of the cycle, rigid, one weight per vertex
3 outnot drawnnot simulated past the walk along its path

The table is read at K = 16; each level resamples the radii by angle and drops stations, which keeps every level's topology fixed and its weights computable. HumanParams.detail (0.25 to 2, as for the car) scales level 0. The quality preset sets the distance of each band and the largest number of figures at levels 0 and 1; past it, the nearest keep their level and the rest drop one. The builder measures on the bench route where the frame time goes and sets the presets' numbers from that.

12In Contravía

  • Ambient crowd. People spawn on the pavements and aprons walks.py lays, more where blocks are dense, and walk along them. Each draws a HumanParams and an outfit from a seed. They step off when the player's car comes up the pavement.
  • NPCs are the same figures with a fixed seed and a place.
  • The player on foot uses one figure the player can keep; walking and running only.
  • Building off the frame. Figures build on the builder thread garage.py already has, cached by their params; a figure that is not ready yet is not shown.

13What the spike showed

Done on 2026-11-21 as a throwaway script outside the repo; the extraction in slice 1 replaces it.

  • klein. The first prompt gave arms against the body. Asking for "a wide gap of white background between each arm and the torso" gave a clean A-pose. Back and profile drawn from the chosen front came out right on the first try; the profile looks left and is mirrored for Pixal3D. Kept seeds: female front 11, male front 12, backs 22, profiles 21.
  • Pixal3D took 266 s (female) and 174 s (male). Outer shells: 70 772 and 133 398 faces, plus an inner shell and 7 to 28 loose bits, removed by visibility.
  • The loft from the sections, at K = 16: 5 312 to 5 376 triangles. The female base reads close to its Pixal3D mesh. Four variants (base, heavy, slim and tall, broad) came from one table.
  • Faults: steps at the hips and bulges at the shoulders where tubes overlap (section 06 replaces them), helmet heads (section 05), and parameters that were loose scales (section 07).

14Delivery

One manifoldx issue per slice, each a PR with renders next to the Pixal3D reference at the same angle; then one Contravía issue.

  1. manifoldx: section extraction. tools/human_sections.py in numpy; the two tables committed; renders of the bare lofts.
  2. manifoldx: continuous skin and head. Crotch fan, shoulder strip, head grid. Closed mesh, constant face count.
  3. manifoldx: HumanParams. The dataclass, interpolation between tables, Proportions mapping, smooth girth curves; a chapter in the book like chapter 17's car.
  4. manifoldx: blended skinning. Second joint and weight in skinning.py and the shader; weights from the table; the 19-joint skeleton.
  5. manifoldx: clothes, hair, face. Offset rings, zones, presets for the eight reference people.
  6. manifoldx: cycles and detail levels. Idle, walk, run, cross; levels 0 to 2 and detail.
  7. contravia: pedestrians. Crowd on sidewalks, NPCs, the player on foot, built on the garage's thread; distance bands and figure counts in the quality presets.

15Testing

  • Topology: for a grid of HumanParams (every range at its ends and middle), the mesh is closed and has the same vertex and face counts.
  • Weights: every vertex's two weights sum to 1; a rigid geometry renders as before.
  • Ranges: the same checks as tests/modeling/test_ranges.py for CarParams.
  • Shape: silhouettes of the base loft against the Pixal3D mesh from front and side, IoU over a threshold the first PR sets.
  • Bends: render elbow and knee at 0, 45 and 90 degrees and check no triangle flips.
  • Cost: the bench route with a crowd at each preset, before and after, per know-how benchmarking; each preset keeps its target frame rate.
  • Levels: every level of every test body is closed and keeps its face count across HumanParams.

16Non-goals and open questions

Not in this spec

  • Children, and the car hitting pedestrians.
  • Getting in and out of the car, pushing, carrying, talking; fingers; facial animation; cloth or hair simulation.
  • Projecting klein's drawings onto the mesh as textures: colour is by zone and decal.

Open questions

None left from the maintainer. The builder sets the girth curves, the stitch layout and the presets' distance bands from measurements.
