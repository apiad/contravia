Vehicles: generic builders in manifoldx and a fleet of eras in Contravía

docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-vehicles-design.html·engine: gia-uh/manifoldx

Every vehicle in the city comes from four parametric builders in manifoldx: the car, extended for the 1950s and the 1980s, and new ones for buses, trucks and cycles. Contravía keeps only a generator that draws their parameters from ranges per era, so the streets fill with dozens of different almendrones, Ladas, modern cars, buses, bicitaxis, tricycles, police cars and fire trucks.

Generic vehicles

manifoldx gains the parts every era needs (bumpers, grilles, lamp styles, hubcaps, fins, two-tone roofs, liveries, lightbars) and three new builders. Nothing in them names Havana.

A fleet of eras

Contravía draws variants from ranges per era, each seeded, each with a few colourways, and reviews them on one generated sheet.

Traffic that looks lived in

The traffic gives each vehicle a variant by its id, so the same car keeps its look along its trip, and the pools grow from 5 kinds to 10.

02Where we start

The four four-wheel references were modelled with today's CarParams, measured from their klein side views (script in .playground/contravia/car/vehicles.py). In each sheet the klein reference is on the left and the model on the right: three-quarter, left side, front, rear. The sedans get their three-box profile; everything that tells them apart is missing, and the bus comes out as a long van.

Pixal3D on the GPU machine was tried first for the almendrón. Its textured mesh matched the drawing, but it is 146,000 faces in 94 overlapping pieces, and neither direct decimation nor a voxel solid gave clean low-poly facets. That route is dropped for vehicles.

04How it fits

The fleet generator calls the builders with the parameters it draws; Vehicle.merged turns each variant into one vertex-coloured mesh; contravia/vehicles.py keeps one pool per variant and colourway, as it keeps one pool per kind today. The meshes are cached across hot reloads through contravia/kept.py, as today.

Each module returns a Vehicle: parts with materials and pivots, pose(...) for doors, wheels and steering (a cycle's handlebar steers its front wheel), and landmarks. They share the car's wheel, lamp, decal and livery code instead of copying it. Riders are not part of a cycle: they are people, with their own spec.

07The fleet generator

contravia/rules/fleet.py holds, per era, a range for every parameter it varies, the probability of each style option, and a colour list. variant(era, k) seeds a generator from the era's name and k, draws a parameter set, and fixes it so it is buildable: the wheelbase fits inside the length, the heights stay in order (sill, belt, cowl, roof), the wheels fit their arches. The same era and k give the same car on every machine. Each variant has three colourways from its era's list.

A kind holds several variants, each a pool. The traffic gives a vehicle its variant from its id (hash(id) mod variants, weighted by each era's share), so the flow layer and the near traffic agree. Default pool sizes and era shares are settings in contravia/rules/traffic.py, overridable with --traffic my.json, not facts about Havana; the bench decides how many variants a kind can afford.

10Testing

  • manifoldx: every option and builder gets tests in tests/modeling/ for its bounding box against its parameters, its part names, its pose (a door opens about its hinge, a wheel turns about its axle) and that every lamp and decal lands on the face it names. A decal that misses its face is an error, not a red smear.
  • Review sheets: contravia fleet-sheet renders every variant in four views; the klein references sit next to the sedans, the bus and the cycles. A person approves each sheet before its PR merges.
  • contravia: variant(era, k) is stable (same parameters twice), every drawn parameter is inside its range, and every variant builds.
  • contravia bench before and after step 8: frame times, triangles and draw calls in traffic.

11Non-goals and open questions

  • Not in this spec: drivers and riders, damage, interiors seen from inside, the articulated bus, and the player's own car choice.
  • Question 1: real proportions, or the klein sheets' taller toy look? The spec carries one height factor so either is one number.
  • Question 2: flat shading on every vehicle, as branding.md asks? It also changes cars that already use car.py elsewhere unless it stays an option.
  • Question 3: how many variants per kind? A start of 8 per car era and 3 colourways is 72 car meshes; the bench decides.
  • Question 4: the moto family. Today's "moto" is a narrow car; the spec builds it with cycle.py. The MZ and the electric moto need their references checked (the MZ front sheet has extra wheels).
