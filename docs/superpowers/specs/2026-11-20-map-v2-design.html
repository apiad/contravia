Map v2: a map that looks finished and moves smoothly

spec·extends 2026-11-18-city-map-design and 2026-11-19-map-sea-coast-design

The map works but reads as a prototype: tiles of different tint, dark streaks on the coast, a crowd of identical black icons, a panel cut by the screen, a camera that jumps with each wheel step and streets that break at altitude. This spec fixes all six. It adds illustrated city emblems and draws the streets on top of the relief at a fixed width in pixels.

  • The ground has one colour and one light whatever level of the quadtree draws it: no lighter rectangles, no dark streaks on the coast, no dither left behind by a crossfade.
  • Zoom, pan and turn ease with inertia. The frame time on the map stays within the drive's budget (20 ms) while far views load. The coarser level stays drawn and the finer one fades in, as manifoldx's QuadStreamer already does.
  • From the whole island down to about 13 km there is always something to read: medallions for the big cities, dots for towns, names that never overlap. Below that the real geometry takes over.
  • Streets read as crisp lines at any altitude, drawn over the relief, with a width in screen pixels set by the road's width and the zoom.
  • The panel on the right always fits the screen.

03The ground

Rectangles of another tint

Each summary node bakes its colours at its own level: tint(colour, heights, L) mixes the height tint by L, shade(colour, normals, L) darkens slopes by L, and the normals are stretched by exaggeration(L). When a level-7 node sits beside a level-9 node, their tint and light differ, and the seam shows as a lighter rectangle.

The fix bakes what does not depend on the camera: the cover colour, the height and the normal unstretched. A map shader input, set once a frame from mapview.exaggeration(h) and the camera height, does the height tint, the slope shade and the stretch for every node alike. If manifoldx's StandardMaterial cannot take that input, the fallback bakes all levels with one fixed tint and shade, the island view's, and fades them in by camera height through the material's tint. SUMMARY_VERSION goes to 6 and summary/ (127 MB) is rebuilt on load.

Dark streaks on the north coast, dither left behind, shallows shifted south

These three are diagnosed in the slice before any fix: the skirts and the 800 m shallows under 20× relief, a crossfade's 4×4 pattern that stays when a node is undrawn mid-fade, and the shelf drawn at _shelf_y under a camera far from it. Each fix comes with a shot at the height where the fault showed and the same shot after.

04Motion

The map keeps two cameras: the target the input moves and the camera drawn, which follows the target each frame with a critically damped spring on x, z, log h and yaw (time constant about 0.12 s). Steady frames depend on the second part: nothing on the main thread may cost more than a few milliseconds in a map frame.

InputMoves the target
Wheel steplog h by a fixed step, about 0.18 a notch, toward the point under the pointer.
W / Slog h at a steady rate, eased in and out over 0.15 s, on the crosshair.
DragThe ground under the pointer follows it exactly while held. On release the pan keeps its speed and decays with a time constant of about 0.35 s.
Arrows, Q / EPan and turn speeds eased in and out over 0.15 s.
ENTER on a group, flightThe target jumps; the follow makes it a glide.

Work that leaves the frame: the marks are projected once per camera change, not per frame, and no Image.svg or text changes unless its content changed. The whereis lookups already run on a thread. Frame time is measured on the map before and after each slice with the same scripted flight (island → Oriente → Santiago → back) and reported as median and worst frame.

05City emblems

Medallion

Provincial capitals and every city over 100 000 people. A coloured disc with an ink silhouette, a paper ring and an ink rim, with a drop shadow. Radius s·(11 + 19·√(pop / 1.75 M)) px, where s grows from 1 to 1.8 as the camera comes down. Own glyphs: Capitolio (La Habana, yellow), cathedral (Santiago), tinajón (Camagüey), Loma de la Cruz (Holguín), Che monument (Santa Clara), mogote (Pinar del Río), bridges (Matanzas), Yunque (Baracoa), tower (Trinidad). The rest: a generic town profile. Accents cycle coral, turquoise, mustard, mint, sky.

Names

Cities in the brand's condensed black italic, uppercase, paper with an ink outline; La Habana in yellow. Towns: a paper dot with an ink ring and the name in condensed bold. Placement in two passes by population: first the symbols, leaving out any that would overlap one placed; then the names, trying below (medallions) or right, left, above, below. A town whose name does not fit loses its dot.

By height

Over 600 km: cities over 140 000. Over 200 km: every medallion and towns over 40 000. Over 80 km: towns over 15 000. Below: every town. Under about 13 km the emblems fade out and the street names of the real geometry take over. An emblem that appears or leaves fades over 0.25 s; sizes change in 6 steps with a crossfade.

Collectible places

From the island to the city stage the black icons go. Each medallion carries a yellow chip with the number of places within 12 km (★43 on Santiago). Single icons come back under 13 km, at 20 px. ENTER on a medallion comes down over its city, as on a group today.

06Streets on top

manifoldx gets a line pass: world-space polylines with a per-vertex colour and a width in screen pixels, expanded to quads in the vertex shader, drawn after the opaque pass with depth test on against a biased depth (a share of the view distance), anti-aliased at the edges. It honours Camera.vertical_scale, so the lines sit on the stretched relief.

In the map, the roads of the drawn summary nodes leave the summary mesh and go to this pass: width in pixels from the road's width and the height, from 1 px for a local road seen from the island to 4 px for the autopista, in road colours with a thin dark casing. Below about 2 km they fade out and the cells' own streets take over. While driving nothing changes. The pass lands in manifoldx main first, then contravia locks it (uv lock --upgrade-package manifold-gfx, uv sync --inexact) and the game restarts once, when the maintainer says so.

07The panel

The panel on the right never leaves the screen: its rows are capped by the height left under the title (at most 10 at the island stage, not 25), names are cut to the panel's width, and it keeps 40 px from the screen edges at every window size.

09Testing

  • mapview: the follow reaches the target without overshoot; a wheel step toward the pointer keeps the ground point under it.
  • Emblems: placement leaves no two boxes overlapping; the height rules pick the counts of the mock's four views.
  • cover: the colour of a point does not depend on the level that bakes it.
  • manifoldx: the line pass draws a line N px wide at any distance, and a ridge in front hides it.
  • Shots of the four mock views after each slice, and the scripted flight's median and worst frame.

10Non-goals

  • 3D city models, search on the map, route planning.
  • New terrain or cover data; no downloads.
  • Changes to the drive view.
