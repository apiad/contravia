# Trademarks

"Contravía" and the Contravía logo identify the official game and the official store. The licences in this
repository cover code, data and assets; they do not grant any right to the name or the logo.

- Forks may say they are "based on Contravía", but must ship under another name and without the logo.
- Mods follow clause 2c of `MODS.md`: they present themselves as "a mod for Contravía", without the logo and without
  looking official.
- Videos, streams, reviews and articles may use the name to talk about the game.
